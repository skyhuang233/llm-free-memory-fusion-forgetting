3.1 Persona
We select an initial persona statement p_{c} from the MSC dataset~\cite{bib.bib58} encompassing 4 to 5 sentences and employ gpt-3.5-turbo as \{M} to expand these into full persona statement p (See examples and prompt details in Appendix A.1[ref_id]A1.SS1). The generated statements typically include details about one or more of the following elements~\cite{bib.bib13}: objectives past experiences daily habits and interpersonal relationships as well as name age and gender of the individual.
