Temporal Reasoning Performance
Temporal reasoning tasks hinge on accurate modeling of event sequences their relative ordering and durations within conversational history. Our architectures demonstrate substantial improvements across all metrics with \texttt{Mem0}^{\tiny g}achieving the highest F1(51.55) and J(58.13) suggesting that structured relational representations in addition to natural language memories significantly aid in temporally grounded judgments. Notably the base variant Mem0 also provide a decent Jscore (55.51) suggesting that natural language alone can aid in temporally grounded judgments. Among baselines OpenAI notably underperforms with scores below 15% primarily due to missing timestamps in most generated memories despite explicit prompting in the OpenAI ChatGPT to extract memories with timestamps. Other baselines such as A-Mem achieve respectable results yet our models clearly advance the state-of-the-art emphasizing the critical advantage of accurately leveraging both natural language contextualization and structured graph representations for temporal reasoning.
