3.4 Human Verification & Editing
In the concluding phase human annotators are tasked with (1) editing the dialogue to eliminate long-term inconsistencies (2) removing or substituting irrelevant images and (3) verifying and editing for alignment between event graphs and the content of the conversations. Overall we observed that annotators edited nearly 15% of the dialog turns and removed or substituted approx. 19% images present in the LLM-generated dataset. See examples of some edits in Appendix A.3[ref_id]A1.SS3.
