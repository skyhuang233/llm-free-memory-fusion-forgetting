Synthetic Evaluation Benchmark.
 Faced with a shortage of human-generated data and observing that LLMs are approaching the quality of human-level annotations~\cite{bib.bib16 bib.bib27} there has been a surge in research drawing inspiration from this development. Consequently numerous studies have started utilizing LLMs to augment or synthesize large-scale dialogue benchmarks for assessing responses in everyday social interactions~\cite{bib.bib23} examining responses in multi-modal environment~\cite{bib.bib12} and evaluating responses that align with specific persona~\cite{bib.bib20}. We leverage LLMs to create data but ensure its high quality with human verification and editing.
