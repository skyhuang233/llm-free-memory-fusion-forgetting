\subsection{Targeted error analysis}

Across the inspected model logs, DFM-Fusion underperforms LLM-Fusion in two recurring ways. The dominant failure mode is temporal retrieval miss: the retrieved evidence is either absent or incomplete, after which LLM-Fusion sometimes still answers with a coarse relative date such as ``last Tuesday'' or ``last week.'' A second, smaller failure mode appears in open-domain questions that require paraphrastic synthesis rather than verbatim evidence packing. These patterns match the category-level averages in Table~\ref{tab:main_results} (lower block), where LLM-Fusion retains small advantages on temporal and open-domain questions.

\subsection{DeepSeek-V3.2 single-model sanity check}

Before running the six-model family study, we validated the harness using \texttt{DeepSeek-V3.2} (API, temperature=0) as the answer model, with 3 independent runs per condition. These results confirmed the directional ordering (DFM-Fusion $>$ No-Fusion, DFM-Fusion $\approx$ LLM-Fusion) and are included only as a harness sanity check; the main empirical basis of the paper is the six-model local-model evaluation in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}.
